# Cayo Drinks — Customer Personas & Segmentation

## Target Personas

### 1. "The Wellness Professional" — Ama (29, Management Consultant, Airport Residential)
- **Habits**: Hits the gym 4 mornings a week, eats clean, works long hours in tech/consulting.
- **Pain Point**: Needs healthy, natural hydration that isn't loaded with artificial cane sugar or chemical preservatives.
- **Cayo Solution**: Orders 6-packs of Island Breeze, Citrus Rush, and Guava Glow for weekly fridge stock via web app using Mobile Money.

### 2. "The Event & Culture Enthusiast" — Kwesi (26, Creative Director, Osu)
- **Habits**: Attends gallery openings, pop-ups, music listening parties, and weekend beach excursions.
- **Pain Point**: Wants trendy, visually striking drinks for gatherings that look great on social media and taste exceptional.
- **Cayo Solution**: Orders Berry Splash and Midnight Passion cocktail cans for sundowner sessions.

### 3. "The Event Planner / Host" — Efua (34, Luxury Wedding & Corporate Planner, East Legon)
- **Habits**: Coordinates 30+ upscale weddings, corporate galas, and private villa dinners per year.
- **Pain Point**: Needs reliable, visually stunning bar catering with flawless presentation and transparent accounting.
- **Cayo Solution**: Books the Cayo Mobile Bar with custom-branded PET cans and uniformed craft mixologists.
