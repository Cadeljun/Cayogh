# Cayo Drinks — Business Model & Structure

## Business Model Canvas

### 1. Value Propositions
- Convenient on-demand delivery of 100% natural cold-pressed fruit drinks and bar-quality cocktails.
- Transparent, hygienic PET can canning technology preserving freshness and visual beauty.
- Hassle-free mobile bar catering for weddings, high-end parties, and corporate galas.

### 2. Revenue Streams
- **D2C E-Commerce**: Direct individual and bundle sales via web app and WhatsApp (Paystack Mobile Money & Cards).
- **Corporate Catering & Subscriptions**: Weekly recurring juice deliveries for corporate offices, banks, and creative studios in Accra.
- **Event Bar Services**: Fixed-rate bar setups + per-drink consumption packages for private events.
- **B2B Wholesale / Consignment**: Supplying boutique hotels, beach clubs, and upscale grocers across Accra.

### 3. Key Resources
- Cold-press extraction and automated can-seaming equipment.
- Sourcing relationships with organic fruit farms in Ghana.
- Central production kitchen & cold storage facility in Accra.
- Digital ordering infrastructure with integrated Paystack payments and WhatsApp dispatch.

### 4. Key Partnerships
- Local farmer cooperatives and produce aggregators.
- Event planners, wedding coordinators, and hospitality concierges.
- Payment gateway (Paystack Ghana) and local courier dispatch networks.
