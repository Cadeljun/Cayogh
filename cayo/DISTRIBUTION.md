# Cayo Drinks — Distribution & Logistics

## Logistics Footprint (Greater Accra)

### 1. Direct-to-Consumer On-Demand Delivery
- **Coverage Zones**:
  - *Zone 1 (Core — 30-45 mins)*: Airport Residential, Cantonments, Labone, Osu, Ridge, Dzorwulu, Roman Ridge.
  - *Zone 2 (Extended — 45-60 mins)*: East Legon, Spintex, Adjiringanor, Abelemkpe, North Kaneshie.
  - *Zone 3 (Outskirts — Scheduled delivery)*: Tema, Sakumono, Kasoa, Kokrobite, Weija.
- **Delivery Fleet**: Network of dedicated motorcycle riders equipped with custom insulated thermal top-boxes loaded with reusable ice packs.

### 2. Retail Consignment & Stockist Model
- Cayo places branded compact glass-door reach-in refrigerators in select partner venues:
  - Boutique fitness clubs & Pilates studios.
  - Premium coworking lounges & executive suites.
  - Artisanal bakeries & organic cafes.
- Restocked 3 times weekly to guarantee product rotation and freshness within shelf-life.

### 3. Events & Pop-Up Logistics
- Mobile refrigerated van transporting draft cocktail kegs, ice chests, glassware, and crates of pre-canned drinks to venue locations.
