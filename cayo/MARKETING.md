# Cayo Drinks — Marketing & Growth Strategy

## Core Acquisition Channels

### 1. Visual & Cultural Social Media
- **Instagram (`@_cayodrinks`)**:
  - Focus on macro visual textures: slow-motion cold press pours, ice-cracking ASMR, behind-the-scenes orchard visits in Ghana.
  - Micro-influencer partnerships with Accra-based fitness trainers, foodies, and fashion creators.
- **TikTok (`@cayodrinks`)**:
  - Trend-jacking, street taste tests in Osu and Labadi, bartender tutorials, and viral "day in the life of a craft beverage founder in Accra".

### 2. High-Visibility Experiential Sampling
- Pop-up complimentary hydration stations at 5K runs, fitness bootcamps, and yoga retreats in Accra.
- Gifting chilled 4-packs to creative studio teams, bridal party suites, and visiting diaspora tastemakers during festive seasons.

### 3. Referral & Community Loops
- "Share the Refreshment": Customers receive a free can on their next order when a friend completes their first purchase via Paystack.
- WhatsApp Broadcast list for VIP drop announcements, secret weekend cocktail flavors, and flash discounts.

### 4. Search & Local SEO
- Optimization for high-intent search terms: "fresh juice delivery Accra", "event cocktail caterer Ghana", "cold pressed fruit drinks Accra", "wedding mobile bar Accra".
