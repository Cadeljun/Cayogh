# Cayo Drinks — Brand Engagement

## Engagement Framework
Cayo engages its community across multiple tiers of involvement:

### 1. Daily Rituals (Digital D2C)
- Quick-order lunchtime hydration via WhatsApp and web app.
- Office corporate fruit packs for creative agencies and tech startups in Accra.
- Weekend cocktail pre-orders for home dinners and backyard socials.

### 2. Event Mixology & Experience
- "Cayo Pop-Up Bars" featuring live mixologists crafting bespoke cocktails and pouring ice-cold cans at beach lounges, weddings, and music gatherings.
- Limited edition seasonal collaborations with local guest bartenders and chefs.

### 3. Community & Lifestyle Content
- Behind-the-scenes farm visits showing mango and pineapple harvesting.
- Mixology masterclasses on TikTok and Instagram Reels showing how to garnish and pair Cayo juices at home.
- Interactive flavor voting polls where the community votes on the next seasonal release (e.g., Starfruit Spark vs Passion Coconut).

### 4. VIP & Loyalty Rewards
- Early access to seasonal limited batches for frequent buyers.
- Exclusive event invites and customized Cayo merch (insulated tumblers, beach totes, branded bar mats).
