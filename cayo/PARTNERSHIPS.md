# Cayo Drinks — Strategic Partnerships

## Partnership Categories

### 1. Agricultural & Farmer Alliances
- Direct supply contracts with cooperative mango, pineapple, and ginger farmers in Ghana's Central, Volta, and Eastern regions.
- Guaranteed minimum floor pricing to protect farming families against harvest price crashes.
- Sourcing zero-waste byproducts: spent fruit pulp donated or sold to local sustainable animal feed producers.

### 2. Hospitality & Event Collaborations
- **Luxury Wedding Planners**: Preferred beverage supplier status with top event planners in Accra.
- **Beach Clubs & Resorts**: Seasonal pop-up bar takeovers at Kokrobite, Ada Foah, and Busua.
- **Cultural Festivals**: Official craft beverage sponsor for contemporary art, music, and fashion showcases (e.g., AfroFuture, Chale Wote).

### 3. Corporate Wellness Programs
- Partnerships with tech hubs, finance firms, and multinationals in Accra for weekly Monday morning fresh juice deliveries and Friday mixer cocktail bars.

### 4. Technology & Infrastructure
- **Paystack Ghana**: Seamless Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AT) and card transaction gateway with real-time webhook verification.
- **Local Dispatch Networks**: Dedicated courier fleet integration for rapid order fulfillment.
