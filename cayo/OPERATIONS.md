# Cayo Drinks — Production & Operations

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### 1. Sourcing & Quality Inspection
- Daily early-morning arrivals of fresh whole fruit from contracted farming clusters in Central and Eastern regions.
- Inspection criteria: Brix sugar level checks, skin integrity, aroma, and ripeness grading. Defective produce is rejected or composted.

### 2. Washing, Sanitization & Prep
- Triple-bath sanitization cycle:
  1. Filtered water soak to remove surface soil.
  2. Mild ozone or food-grade organic sanitizing rinse.
  3. Final pure reverse-osmosis water rinse.
- Peeling, coring, and dicing in temperature-controlled cold prep room (18°C).

### 3. Cold-Press Extraction
- Commercial hydraulic cold-press juicer operating under high pressure without heat generation to protect vitamins and live enzymes.
- Fine stainless steel mesh filtration to remove excessive fibrous pulp while preserving velvety mouthfeel.

### 4. Canning & Can Seaming
- PET cans pre-rinsed with deionized water.
- Liquid dispensed via automated volumetric filler.
- Nitrogen dosing or minimum headspace fill to purge oxygen.
- Immediate semi-automatic rotary can-seamer application of aluminum lids.
- Tamper-proof batch coding stamp (production date + best-before date).

### 5. Cold Storage & Distribution
- Instant blast chilling down to 3°C.
- Stored in walk-in refrigeration units.
- Packed in insulated thermal bags with frozen ice packs for bike and van dispatch across Accra.
