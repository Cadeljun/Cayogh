# Cayo Drinks — Brand Look & Feel

## Aesthetic Philosophy
**"Tropical Minimalism meets Afro-Chic Luxury."**
Cayo combines the lush warmth of Ghanaian coastlines with sleek, high-contrast modern design. The visual experience avoids cliché tiki tropes, opting instead for sharp typography, deep tropical emeralds, vibrant sunrise oranges, and frosted crystal textures that reflect condensation on cold glass.

## Color Palette
- **Cayo Orange (`#FF5E1E` / `hsl(18 100% 56%)`)**: Primary brand color. Evokes sunrises over Kokrobite, ripe mango nectar, and warm energy.
- **Deep Tropical Noir (`#0A0A0A` / `hsl(0 0% 4%)`)**: Grounding midnight background. Provides dramatic contrast for saturated fruit juices.
- **Emerald Mist (`#10B981`)**: Accent for botanical, herbal, and freshness cues (fresh mint, lime zest, moringa hints).
- **Golden Hour Sun (`#F59E0B`)**: Secondary warm accent representing aged rum, golden pineapples, and premium status.
- **Pure Cloud (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`)**: Clean editorial typography and high-legibility interface elements.

## Typography
- **Headlines / Display**: *Poppins* (Weights: 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold). Modern geometric sans-serif with round, friendly curves that mimic fruit silhouettes.
- **Body & Editorial**: *Inter* (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold). High x-height, neutral readability for digital ordering and product descriptions.
- **Accents & Code**: Monospaced fonts for receipt verification, batch dates, and freshness stamps.

## Packaging & Product Form Factor
- **Crystal PET Cans**: Transparent food-grade PET cans with silver aluminum easy-open ends (EOE). The natural color of the juice serves as the hero graphic.
- **Frosted Minimalist Sleeves**: Semi-opaque wrap labels with clean UV spot varnishing, emphasizing ingredients and nutritional honesty.
- **Glass Bottle Reserve (Cocktails)**: Amber and flint heavy-bottom apothecary bottles with natural cork or tamper-evident seals for aged blends and signature event cocktails.

## Photography & Art Direction
- **Lighting**: Bright, directional natural sunlight or dramatic high-contrast studio strobes highlighting condensation beads, ice facets, and liquid movement.
- **Angles**: Macro product shots at eye-level; dynamic overhead pour shots; lifestyle shots featuring diverse young professionals, beachgoers, creatives, and wedding attendees in Accra.
- **Textures**: Splashes of water, sliced fresh dragonfruit, crushed ice, palm leaf shadows, and brushed aluminum.
