# Cayo Drinks — Strategic Roadmap (2026–2028)

## Three-Horizon Strategy

### Horizon 1: Brand Dominance in Accra (Months 1–12)
- Optimize the central production kitchen in Accra with semi-automated cold-press and can-seaming lines.
- Scale direct digital orders via `cayogh.netlify.app` with instant Paystack checkout (MoMo & cards).
- Establish exclusive bar-catering presence at 50 premier private events, destination weddings, and creative festivals.
- Target: 10,000 cans sold per month; break-even achieved by Month 8.

### Horizon 2: Regional Expansion & Modern Retail (Months 13–24)
- Expand delivery coverage across Greater Accra and into Kumasi and Takoradi through refrigerated logistics.
- Place refrigerated Cayo display coolers in 25 select gourmet supermarkets (Marina Mall, Koala, Palace) and boutique gym franchises.
- Introduce ready-to-drink (RTD) cocktail cans in compliant retail formats.
- Target: 40,000 cans sold per month.

### Horizon 3: Pan-African & Diaspora Export (Months 25–36)
- Launch high-pressure processing (HPP) or aseptic pasteurization line for extended shelf-life (45–60 days) without flavor compromise.
- Pilot distribution into Lagos, Abidjan, and London/UK specialty Afro-Caribbean luxury food distributors.
- Explore franchise or license models for Cayo Beach Bars across coastal West Africa.
