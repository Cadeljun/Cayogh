## B2C Digital Sales
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Target GH₵ 100–150 (3–5 cans per consumer order).
- **Checkout Friction Reduction**: Instant Paystack Mobile Money integration enables 30-second checkout directly on smartphones without entering credit card numbers.
- **Cart Abandonment Recovery**: Automated SMS / WhatsApp reminder with one-click payment link.
