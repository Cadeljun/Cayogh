# Cayo Drinks — Brand Voice & Tone

## Voice Persona: "The Cultured Island Host"
Cayo speaks with the warmth of Ghanaian hospitality, the wit of urban Accra, and the refined polish of a luxury boutique lounge. We are energetic, unpretentious, passionate about great flavor, and deeply rooted in our culture.

## Core Tone Attributes
1. **Refreshing & Uplifting**: Optimistic, sensory, and crisp. We invite customers to pause, breathe, and savor the moment.
2. **Authentic & Honest**: Transparent about ingredients, sourcing, and preparation. No artificial jargon or health-washing.
3. **Playful yet Sophisticated**: Conversational and charming without lapsing into goofy slang. Confident in our quality.
4. **Culturally Grounded**: Proudly Ghanaian with an international lifestyle perspective.

## Language & Copy Rules
- **Do use sensory verbs**: *Muddle, cold-press, swirl, chill, steep, burst, balance, refresh.*
- **Do celebrate local roots**: Reference Accra spots (Osu, Labone, Cantonments, Kokrobite), Ghanaian farmers, and authentic ingredients (calamansi, ginger, hibiscus/sobolo calyces).
- **Avoid corporate or medical claims**: Don't say "detoxifying miracle cure"; say "packed with natural vitamins, real fruit fiber, and revitalizing hydration."
- **Avoid dated cocktail tropes**: No cheesy "party hard" slogans; emphasize craft, curation, connection, and mindful indulgence.

## Channel-Specific Applications
- **Instagram / TikTok**: Snappy, visually driven, conversational hooks ("Your afternoon pick-me-up just landed", "Pure ginger heat meets cold pineapple sweetness").
- **WhatsApp Customer Service**: Warm, prompt, and hospitable ("Good afternoon! Your cold-pressed Berry Splash is on the bike right now with rider Kofi").
- **Packaging Copy**: Clear, elegant, and concise ingredient breakdowns with bottling timestamps.
