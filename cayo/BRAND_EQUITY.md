# Cayo Drinks — Brand Equity

## Drivers of Equity
Cayo builds brand equity by establishing a distinct premium tier in West Africa's beverage ecosystem:

1. **Perceived Quality**:
   - Distinctive crystal PET can packaging creates immediate shelf disruption against standard opaque bottles or generic carton tetra packs.
   - Clean, high-end taste profiles formulated by professional mixologists rather than industrial food chemists.

2. **Brand Associations**:
   - Synonymous with modern Accra cool, beachside wellness, and high-energy celebration.
   - Preferred partner for prestigious creative, fashion, and lifestyle events in Ghana.

3. **Customer Loyalty & Advocacy**:
   - High repeat order rates powered by subscription potential, convenient Mobile Money checkout, and rapid 30-45 minute dispatch in central Accra.
   - High organic user-generated content (UGC) on Instagram stories and TikTok due to aesthetic presentation.

4. **Pricing Power**:
   - Ability to command premium prices (GH₵ 22 - GH₵ 28 for fruit cans; GH₵ 55 - GH₵ 95 for artisanal cocktails) due to perceived freshness, brand status, and artisanal quality.
