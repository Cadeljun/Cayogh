# Cayo Drinks — Market Analysis

## Industry Overview
The beverage sector in Ghana and West Africa is experiencing a seismic transition:
1. **Health & Wellness Shift**: Rising health consciousness among urban middle and upper classes, shifting away from high-sugar carbonated soft drinks toward 100% natural, additive-free juices and functional beverages.
2. **Rise of Afro-Cosmopolitan Lifestyle**: Accra has established itself as a global cultural capital (Afrochella/AfroFuture, Detty December, Year of Return, Chale Wote). Young consumers demand premium, aesthetic lifestyle products that reflect modern African pride.
3. **Cocktail & Mixology Boom**: Growth of cocktail culture in hospitality venues across Osu, Cantonments, Airport City, and Labone.

## Total Addressable Market (TAM), SAM & SOM
- **TAM**: Total non-alcoholic and ready-to-drink (RTD) cocktail market in urban Ghana: ~$120M annually.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**: Premium fresh juices and craft event cocktails in Greater Accra: ~$18M annually.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: Capture 5-8% of Accra's premium D2C and event beverage market over the next 24 months (~$1M–$1.5M).

## Competitive Landscape
- **Mass Industrial Juices (e.g., Ceres, Don Simon)**: Long shelf-life, shelf-stable pasteurized concentrates. Lack freshness, natural taste, and craft appeal.
- **Informal Local Vendors**: Sell sobolo or plastic-bagged juices. High flavor authenticity, but inconsistent hygiene, packaging, and scale.
- **Cayo’s Competitive Advantage**: High-end canning presentation, guaranteed cold-chain hygiene, culinary mixology recipes, seamless digital ordering with Paystack, and dedicated mobile bar execution.
