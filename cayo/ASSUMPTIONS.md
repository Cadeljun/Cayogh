# Cayo Drinks — Key Planning Assumptions & Risk Matrix

## Fundamental Assumptions

### 1. Market & Demand Assumptions
- **Willingness to Pay**: Middle and upper-income consumers in central Accra (Airport, Cantonments, Labone, East Legon) are willing to pay GH₵ 20–28 per 330ml fresh cold-pressed can due to superior health benefits and visual branding.
- **Repeat Cadence**: Wellness consumers will re-order 2–3 times per month if delivery times remain consistently under 45 minutes.
- **Seasonality**: Peak event cocktail demand occurs between November and January ("Detty December" diaspora influx) and April (Easter festivities). Fruit juice demand remains relatively steady year-round with heat-wave spikes.

### 2. Supply Chain & Operations Assumptions
- **Raw Produce Availability**: Local supply of pineapples, watermelons, citrus, and ginger is abundant year-round in southern Ghana. Mangoes experience a primary season (May–July) and a minor season (December–January); off-season mango needs will be met through contracted frozen pulp suppliers.
- **Cold Chain Reliability**: Backup generators and battery inverters are mandatory at central production facilities to counteract local power fluctuations.

### 3. Regulatory & Compliance Assumptions
- Full certification and compliance with Ghana Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) guidelines for food and beverage packaging, labeling, and facility hygiene.
- Regular lab testing for microbial counts, shelf-life validation, and nutritional panels.

## Risk & Mitigation Matrix

| Risk Factor | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fruit Price Volatility** | Medium | Medium | Long-term fixed forward pricing agreements with farmer cooperatives. |
| **Grid Power Outages** | High | High | Dual backup diesel generator + solar-inverter cold room backup. |
| **Perishability / Spoilage** | Medium | High | Daily small-batch pressing based on demand forecasting; zero-waste composting. |
| **Courier Transit Delays** | High | Medium | Clustered zone dispatching; high-spec insulated thermal carrier boxes. |
| **Payment Network Downtime** | Low | Medium | Dual checkout options: Paystack Live + Cash/MoMo on delivery fallback. |
