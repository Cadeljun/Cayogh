# Cayo Drinks — Brand Presence

## Ecosystem Architecture
Cayo maintains an omnichannel footprint spanning physical pop-ups, premier hospitality partnerships, on-demand direct-to-consumer (D2C) web commerce, and social communities.

## Touchpoints
1. **Digital Flagship (`cayogh.netlify.app` / Web App)**:
   - Modern, mobile-first responsive storefront.
   - Real-time catalog of cold-pressed fruit drinks and signature cocktails.
   - Paystack-powered payments (MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Visa/Mastercard).
   - Instant WhatsApp dispatch coordination and interactive order tracking.

2. **Social Channels**:
   - **Instagram (`@_cayodrinks`)**: Aesthetic imagery, behind-the-scenes juice pressing, cocktail recipes, mixology reels, and lifestyle influencer features.
   - **TikTok (`@cayodrinks`)**: High-energy drink preparation, sound bites of cracking open ice-cold PET cans, street interviews, and event recaps.
   - **Facebook (`Cayo Drinks`)**: Community updates, event announcements, bulk catering queries, and corporate testimonials.
   - **WhatsApp (`+233 559 412 097`)**: High-touch VIP concierge, bulk event reservations, custom batch orders, and instant support.

3. **Physical & Experiential Touchpoints**:
   - **Cayo Mobile Bar**: Sleek matte-black & brushed timber mobile bar pods deployed at luxury weddings, Detty December festivals, polo matches, private villa parties, and beach retreats.
   - **Stockist Retailers**: Select boutique cafes, high-end gyms, co-working hubs, and specialty grocers across Airport Residential, Cantonments, Labone, and East Legon.
   - **Corporate & Pop-Up Activations**: Branded refrigerated dispensers at tech company hubs, creative agencies, and international conferences in Accra.
