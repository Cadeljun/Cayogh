# **App Name**: Cayo Drinks

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Homepage Sections: Render interactive sections such as Hero, Signature Drinks, Cocktails, 'Why Choose Cayo Drinks' highlights, Events, QR Ordering explanation, Gallery, Social Media links, and a Footer, all populated with vibrant brand content.
- Intuitive Navigation System: Implement a responsive navigation bar with branded logo ('Cayo Drinks') and clear links to key pages: Home, Menu, Events, Gallery, and Contact.
- Product Showcase & Pricing: Display signature fruit drinks (in PET cans) and premium cocktails (in glass cups) with vivid imagery, names, and pricing clearly indicated in Ghana Cedis (GH₵).
- Event Inquiry Form: Provide a user-friendly form for customers to submit inquiries and book Cayo Drinks services for various events such as parties, weddings, and corporate gatherings.
- Interactive Photo Gallery: Present a curated collection of high-quality images showcasing colorful drinks, fresh fruit ingredients, customers enjoying beverages, and behind-the-scenes drink preparation.
- Social Media & Contact Hub: Integrate prominent links to Cayo Drinks' social media profiles (Instagram, TikTok) and essential contact information within the website footer.
- AI-Powered Drink Pairing Tool: Suggest personalized drink pairings or recommendations based on user input for mood, occasion, or preferred flavor profiles.

## Style Guidelines:

- Dark tropical color scheme. Primary accent color: A warm, rich amber gold (#F5AC0F), evoking the brightness of tropical fruits and luxury. Background color: A deep charcoal with subtle warmth (#261E14), providing a sophisticated, dark base that allows bright drink visuals to pop. Accent color: A vibrant, warm red-orange (#E54119), to highlight CTAs and introduce dynamic visual interest.
- Headlines and section titles will use 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for its modern, precise, and contemporary feel. Body text and descriptive content will utilize 'Inter' (sans-serif) for clear readability and a neutral, objective aesthetic, complementing the bold headlines.
- Utilize clean, elegant line-art style icons that relate to tropical ingredients, speedy service, and celebratory events, maintaining a premium and modern brand image.
- A responsive and dynamic layout featuring large, immersive visual hero sections. Drink items will be presented within elegantly rounded cards against a dark background, ensuring clear focus on the product.
- Incorporate smooth, subtle animations and transitions for navigation elements, image carousels, and section reveals, enhancing the premium feel without distracting from content.
